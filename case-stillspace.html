Why This Product Exists

Most mental health apps are built around productivity: streaks, goals, daily habits. They work for people who are already coping. But for someone in a depressive episode — when getting out of bed is a victory — these apps feel like another thing to fail at.

Still Space is a soft place to land. It doesn't push, track, or grade you. It offers morning rituals to ease you into the day, gentle micro-actions matched to how you actually feel, breathing exercises with ambient sounds, and a diary that reframes your small steps as real progress. A companion character sits with you — not as a therapist, but as a quiet presence that says "I'm here."

Key insight: Most mental health apps are built for general wellness — meditation, habit tracking, journaling. None of them adapt their interface to the user's actual emotional state or offer daily rituals that don't feel like obligations. UX Tone & Voice

Every word in Still Space is a design decision. The voice is warm, unhurried, and accepting — as if a caring friend is sitting next to you.

Role: not a therapist, not a coach — a gentle companion who stays with you.

Forbidden patterns
  • "Start your day right"
  • "Pull yourself together"
  • "You can do it!"
  • "Try harder"
  • "Stay positive"
  • "Don't give up"
Permitted patterns
  • "If it works — great"
  • "If not — that's okay"
  • "Let's just try"
  • "No rush"
  • "Just being is already huge work"
  • "You have the right to feel what you feel"

Design Philosophy

Every design decision starts from one question: "Would this feel safe for someone on their worst day?"

Core principles

  • Warmth first: Lavender accents, peach backgrounds, soft grays, rounded shapes, and a hand-drawn companion character. The interface feels like a hug — never pushes, never rushes
  • Gentle structure: Morning rituals, daily thoughts, and self-reflection questions give the day a soft shape — without turning into obligations. One screen, one moment at a time
  • Quiet delight: Heartbeat haptics in breathing mode, soft confetti after completing a self-care action, ambient soundscapes. Celebrations feel earned and intimate
  • No toxic positivity: The app never says "stay positive" or "you've got this." Instead: "If you have no strength today, that's okay. I'll just be here."

Key Screens

Home & Daily Companion

The home screen opens with a gentle mascot and "How do you feel?" — four energy levels (Heavy, Hard, Okay, Good) let you check in without overthinking. Below, three daily cards rotate fresh content: Morning ritual to ease you into the day, Thought of the day for gentle perspective, and A question for you to spark self-reflection. Quick-access cards stay anchored at the bottom: "Just be here with me" for breathing and presence, "Note what you got through" for recording small wins, and "Let it out and let it go" for expressive writing that disappears after release.

Micro-actions

After checking in, Still Space suggests gentle self-care actions calibrated to your energy level and context — filterable by location: At Home, Outside, or At Work. Each card shows a category (For the soul, Breathing & Body, Basic Care...), a warm description, and a low-pressure "I'll try this" button. The tone never commands — it invites. Completing an action saves it to your diary as proof of what you got through.

I'm Staying With You

For moments when you can't do anything at all — a soft breathing circle with heartbeat haptics and ambient soundscapes: Rain, Ocean, Fireplace, Wind, Night. "I'm staying with you. You don't need to do anything right now. Just be with yourself." No timers, no instructions. A quiet link to crisis support appears at the bottom — always accessible, never intrusive.

Diary & Insights

"Look how far you've come." The diary tracks your mood across day, week, month, and 6-month views, surfacing gentle insights — "Usually you feel better mid-month," "Your mood is stable," "You often feel calm." Below the chart, a timeline of completed micro-actions shows "What you got through" — reframing small steps as real achievements. Every entry is proof of your strength.

Building with AI

Still Space was designed and developed with Claude (Anthropic) as a creative and engineering partner. Not replacing design thinking — amplifying it to ship a complete product in 7 days.

What AI enabled

  • Rapid prototyping: From concept sketches to working SwiftUI code in hours, not weeks
  • Content creation at scale: ~150 localized self-care tasks, daily rituals, reflection questions, and thoughts of the day across 3 languages — each carefully crafted for tone and clinical appropriateness
  • Design system as code: Color tokens, typography, spacing, character illustrations — defined once in design, immediately reflected in production code
  • Iteration speed: Testing ideas in real code instead of static mockups — seeing how animations feel, how the companion character responds, how flows connect

What stayed human

  • Product vision: The "why" — understanding what people in emotional crisis actually need, not what apps typically offer
  • Emotional design: Tone of voice, the companion character's personality, the feeling of safety — these require empathy AI can support but not originate
  • Ethical decisions: Privacy-first architecture, crisis resource verification, no gamification of suffering
  • Curation: AI generates options; a designer decides what truly serves the user

Under the Hood

  • SwiftUI — native iOS, zero external dependencies
  • 100% offline-first — optional iCloud sync for backup
  • Zero data collection — no accounts, no analytics, no ads
  • Multilingual — Ukrainian, English, and Russian
  • Accessibility — WCAG AA contrast, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, minimum 44pt touch targets
  • Dark & light themes — warm palette in both modes
